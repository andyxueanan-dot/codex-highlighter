# v1.2.11 — Inline mathematical selections

Fixes highlighting of single KaTeX letters and selections that combine normal text and inline math, such as “第 i 个”. Element-boundary selections now produce valid text offsets, and hidden MathML copies are excluded from new math-containing anchors. Legacy anchors retain their original raw-text model.

New math anchors are source-specific: selecting the second N in a table does not highlight the first N, and removing the selected formula does not transfer its highlight to another occurrence.

Validated with browser fixtures covering N, a, script A, subscripts, hidden MathML selections, mixed prose, DOM replacement, recoloring/deletion, and pre-update anchors. The existing highlight, launcher, retention and large-data-transfer suites also pass. Native restart behavior is unchanged.
